Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Chinese clothing sizes run smaller than US/EU sizes?

Chinese clothing is designed for Asian body proportions, so shoulder width, chest, and sleeve length are generally 1–2 sizes smaller than Western standards. Always check flat measurements before buying.

How does ChinaFit AI convert Taobao and 1688 sizes?

ChinaFit AI takes your personal measurements and compares them with the listing’s size logic to recommend a fit, including notes about platform-specific quirks.

How do I convert Chinese shoe sizes to US or EU sizes?

Chinese shoe sizes use foot length in millimeters. For example, 260 mm ≈ EU 42 / US Men’s 8.5. Also check foot width and the seller’s insole length.

Is a Chinese L the same as a US L?

No. Compare the seller’s centimeter chart to your body. A Chinese L often fits like a US M or S.

Are Chinese shoe sizes the same as EU sizes?

No. Do not treat a Chinese 42 as an EU 42. Use foot length in millimeters.
