Taobao Clothing Size Chart: Chinese S/M/L/XL/2XL to Real US Sizes

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · ~6 min read

The shortcut: go up 1–2 letter sizes from your usual US size — or skip letters and match chest. A Chinese L is very often a US Men's M. A Chinese 2XL is closer to a true US L, not a US 2XL.

These charts reflect typical unbranded Taobao / 1688 tops. Designer and athletic brands can differ; when they do, use the seller’s centimeter chart.

Pants: waist numbers vs US

Chinese pants are often tagged 27–34 (inches of garment waist) or by letter. Stretch denim can be 1–2" smaller than the tag. Non-stretch trousers should match your measured waist plus 2–4 cm of ease.

Numeric codes like 170/88A

  • 170 = height in cm the pattern is drafted for.
  • 88 = body chest or bust in cm (not garment chest).
  • A / B / C = drop / body type (A is average).

A 175/96A shirt is aimed at someone ~175 cm tall with a 96 cm chest — roughly a US Men's S/M, even if the shop labeled it XL.

Learn the centimeter fields in how to read 平铺胸围, 衣长, and 肩宽. Shoes use a different system: CN / mm to US.

FAQ

Is Asian sizing always two sizes down?

For cheap unbranded Taobao tees, yes — plan on 1–2 sizes. For Uniqlo China, Nike, or Dewu authentic, use the brand’s own US/EU chart instead.

What if two listings both say XL but look different?

That is normal. Compare 平铺胸围. A 54 cm XL and a 58 cm XL are different garments that happen to share a letter.
