How to Read Chinese Clothing Measurements (Flat Chest, Length, Shoulder)

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · ~7 min read

US size charts usually list your body. Taobao charts usually list the garment laid flat. That single difference is why a Chinese “L” feels like a US “M” — and why letter sizes are a trap.

Ignore S/M/L until you have decoded three numbers: 平铺胸围 (flat chest), 衣长 (length), and 肩宽 (shoulder width).

Flat chest vs US chest: the conversion that matters

1. Measure your body chest at the fullest point (cm).
2. Convert the seller’s 平铺胸围: garment chest = flat × 2.
3. You want the garment to be 4–10 cm larger than your body for a normal top (less for slim knits, more for oversized).

Example: you are a US Men's M with a 100 cm chest. A Chinese L with 52 cm flat chest (104 cm around) is the realistic match — not the Chinese M at 50 cm flat.

Shoulder width: why US shoppers feel squeezed

East Asian patterns assume a narrower shoulder and a shorter armscye. Even when chest circumference is OK, 肩宽 can be 1–3 cm tighter than a US garment in the “same” size.

US men's size Typical US shoulder Typical Chinese 肩宽 (same letter)
S43–44 cm41–42 cm
M45–46 cm43–44 cm
L47–48 cm45–46 cm
XL49–51 cm47–48 cm

Fix: measure a well-fitting shirt seam-to-seam and match 肩宽 within ±1 cm. Broad shoulders should size by shoulder first, then check chest ease.

衣长 (length) vs what you expect

  • Asian tees and shirts often sit shorter in the body than US cuts at the same chest.
  • Compare 衣长 to a garment in your closet, not to a generic “US L is 29 inches” rule.
  • Measure from the highest shoulder point next to the collar, straight down to the hem.

How to measure yourself (and a garment you own)

  1. Chest (body): tape around the fullest part, level, not flexed.
  2. Flat chest (garment): lay it flat, measure pit to pit. Do not stretch knits.
  3. Shoulder: back, seam to seam, following the shoulder line.
  4. Length: high shoulder to hem.

Ask the seller: “请提供平铺胸围、肩宽、衣长（厘米）” — please send flat chest, shoulder, and length in cm.

FAQ

Is 胸围 already doubled?

If the number is in the 90–120 cm range for adult tops, it is likely full circumference. If it is 46–62 cm, it is almost certainly flat (平铺).

Can I use US letter size on Taobao?

Not safely. Treat letters as decoration. Two “XL” listings can differ by 6+ cm in chest.
