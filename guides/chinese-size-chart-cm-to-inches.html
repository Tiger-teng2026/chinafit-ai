The Size Chart Is in Centimeters — I Think in Inches

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · ~4 min read

A first-time Shein or Taobao shopper sees “chest 96 cm” and thinks, “I’m a 36-inch chest.” They skip the math, pick a usual M, and cannot button the shirt. That is the first wall every Western buyer hits: metric vs imperial.

It is not China’s fault — most of the world uses centimeters. Formula: 1 inch = 2.54 cm. So 36 inches × 2.54 ≈ 91.5 cm. Too lazy to calculate? Search “36 inches to cm” — or better, stop converting every time.
