What Does 160/84A Mean on Chinese Clothing Labels?

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · ~5 min read

You flip the tag, and there it is: 160/84A. What does that even mean? The first time many US shoppers order from a Chinese site, they ignore the code, pick an L, and end up with a shirt they cannot get an arm through. Chinese sizing is not just smaller — it speaks a different language.

China’s national standard (GB/T 1335) uses height + chest/waist + body type. So 160/84A means: 160 cm tall (about 5'3"), 84 cm chest (for tops) or waist (for pants), and “A” for average build. Logical — except when you are used to a US 6 or UK 10, those numbers do not ring a bell.

What Y / A / B / C mean

160/84A vs a US size

Search “Chinese size chart to US” and every site gives a slightly different answer. A Chinese M tagged 160/84A might land around a US 4–6 for women. If you only look at the “M”, you will almost certainly buy too small.

Rule: ignore the letter. Measure your chest in centimeters and match the seller’s cm chart. That number system is more accurate than S/M/L — use it right, and it becomes a tool, not an enemy.
