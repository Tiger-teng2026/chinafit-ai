Returning a Wrong-Size Item from China Costs More Than the Item

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · ~5 min read

Pants 5 cm too small in the waist. Return shipping can cost more than the pants. Sending them back makes no sense; keeping them is useless. That pair stays in the closet with the tag on. For overseas buyers, a size miss often means you are stuck. International returns are pricey, slow, and messy. Many sellers do not accept returns from abroad — or the process is painful enough that you give up.

Worse: some charts are inaccurate. You follow the numbers, the garment is still wrong, you leave a review, and move on. Search “return clothes from China” and you will find thousands in the same boat.
