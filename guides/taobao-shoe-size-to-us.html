Shoe Size Guide

Taobao Shoe Size to US: Complete Men's & Women's Chart

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · ~6 min read

Chinese marketplaces label shoes as CN (often the same number as EU) or by foot length in millimeters. A Taobao 42 is not automatically a US 9. Use the charts below: find your foot length in mm, then read across to US Men's or US Women's.

Rule that actually works on Taobao: trust insole length (mm), not the size printed on the box. Unbranded pairs frequently run 5–10 mm short.

How to measure your foot for Taobao

  1. Stand on paper in the socks you will wear with the shoes.
  2. Mark the heel and the longest toe. Measure in millimeters.
  3. Add 5–10 mm of toe room (5 mm for snug sneakers, 10 mm for comfort / wide feet).
  4. Ask the seller for 鞋垫长度 (insole length). Compare that number — not CN — to your target.

Taobao vs Poizon vs branded sizing

  • Taobao / 1688 unbranded: Often runs small. A labeled 260 mm insole can measure 250–255 mm.
  • Poizon / Dewu authentic: Follow the brand's own US/EU chart (Nike, Adidas, New Balance). CN is usually just the EU equivalent.
  • JD.com official stores: Closer to brand-standard. Third-party shops still vary.

Need clothing letter sizes too? See Chinese S/M/L/XL to real US sizes and how to read flat-lay measurements. And don't assume a Chinese 44 is an EU 44 — the numbers look the same, but they're not.

FAQ

What US size is a Chinese 42?

US Men's 8.5 or US Women's 10, for a ~260 mm foot. Confirm insole length before you buy.

Should I size up on Taobao?

For no-name sneakers and boots, yes — many buyers go up a half or full CN size. For authentic Nike/Adidas on Dewu, use the brand chart instead.

Is CN the same as EU?

On most Taobao listings, yes. The reliable figure is still foot/insole length in mm.
