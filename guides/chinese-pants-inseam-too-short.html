Pants — Waist Fits, but the Inseam Is Way Too Short

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · ~5 min read

Measure a 32-inch waist (about 81 cm), find “waist 82 cm,” and feel golden. The pair arrives: waist is spot on, hem hangs three inches above the ankle. Chinese pants are built for shorter legs. Charts often list waist and skip a true inseam — and when they do list length, it is drafted around average Asian height (about 5'7" men, 5'2" women).

If you are 5'10" or taller, standard length is almost certainly too short.

How much shorter?

Typical inseam
Chinese L (men)78–80 cm (≈ 31")
US L82–84 cm (≈ 32–33")
Gap2–4 cm — “perfect” vs high-water

What to do

  • Measure your own inseam: crotch to where you want the hem.
  • Prefer charts with waist and 裤内长 / inseam. 裤长 is often outseam (waist to hem).
  • Waist-only chart: assume short. Look for “tall” or “long” if offered.
  • Read reviews from buyers near your height.

Letter vs waist tags: Chinese S/M/L pants to US.
