Chinese Jin to Lbs: Convert 斤 / 市斤 to Pounds & kg

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · ~5 min read

Taobao groceries, tea, nuts, and many parcel notes use 斤 (jin), not pounds. On the Chinese mainland, that unit is the metric 市斤:

1 jin (市斤) = 500 g = 0.5 kg = 1.102 lbs

Do not confuse 斤 with 公斤 (gongjin), which is a full kilogram (2.205 lbs). Mixing them up doubles or halves the weight you think you ordered.

斤 vs 公斤 vs Hong Kong catty

  • 市斤 / 斤 (mainland Taobao): 500 g. This is what almost every Taobao food listing means.
  • 公斤: 1,000 g. If the title says 2公斤, that is 4 jin / 4.41 lbs — not 2 jin.
  • Hong Kong 斤 (catty): about 604.8 g (1.333 lbs). Not used on mainland Taobao.
  • Taiwan 斤: commonly 600 g. Check the seller's region if you are not on Taobao.

FAQ

How many pounds is 1 jin?

1.10 lbs (1.1023 to be exact). Two jin is 2.20 lbs — the same as 1 kilogram.

How many jin in a pound?

About 0.91 jin per pound. One US pound is 453.6 g; one mainland jin is 500 g.

Why do sellers write “净重 1斤”?

净重 means net weight. 1斤 net = 500 g of product, excluding packaging.
