What in the World Is “Free Size” (均码)?

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · ~4 min read

Cute hoodie, great price, one option: Free Size. It arrives so tight the shoulders cannot move and the hem stops at the belt. 均码 / “One Size” is not for everyone. It is cut for an average Asian body — typically around 5'1"–5'5" and 110–130 lbs. Outside that range, Free Size often means “this will not fit you.”

Sellers like it because fewer sizes mean lower cost and less inventory. Some even use Free Size as a polite label for S or XS.
