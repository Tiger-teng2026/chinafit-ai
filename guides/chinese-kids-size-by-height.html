Kids’ Sizes Are by Height (80, 90, 100…) — Which One?

Last updated: August 27, 2026 · ~4 min read

A US 3T dress for a three-year-old. Chinese options: 80, 90, 100, 110, 120. Those numbers are height in centimeters, not age. A 100 is aimed at a child around 95–105 cm tall — roughly US 3T — but Chinese kids’ clothes also run small. A 100 may only fit a child who is 95 cm, not 100.

Safe play: size up at least one full size. Child is 95 cm? Buy 110. Chunky or broader frame? You may need two sizes up.
