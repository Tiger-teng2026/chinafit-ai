Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

ChinaFit AI (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the website at https://www.chinafitai.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our AI size-conversion service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

ChinaFit AI provides AI-assisted clothing and shoe size recommendations for shoppers buying from Chinese marketplaces such as Taobao, Poizon/Dewu, JD.com, and 1688. For privacy questions, contact us at support@chinafitai.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Body measurements you enter, such as foot length, foot width, height, weight, or waist. These values are used only to generate a size recommendation.
  • Support messages you send to our email, including any details you choose to share.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and basic request logs generated by our hosting provider.
  • Local storage on your device, including a Pro access token after a successful purchase so we can unlock paid features on return visits.

Payment information

Payments for the Lifetime Pro Pass are processed by our payment partner, Creem. We do not collect, store, or have access to your full credit-card number or bank details. Creem may collect billing information needed to complete the transaction under its own privacy policy.

3. How we use your information

  • To generate free size estimates and, for Pro users, DeepSeek AI purchase-protection reports.
  • To verify Pro access after payment and remember that status on your device.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your measurements for advertising profiles.

4. AI processing

If you unlock Pro, the measurements you submit are sent to our backend and then to DeepSeek, our AI provider, so we can generate a detailed sizing report and seller message. That processing is performed solely to deliver the service you requested. AI outputs are estimates for reference only and are not a substitute for checking a seller’s actual product measurements.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage to keep a Pro pass token after checkout. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party ad trackers. Our hosting or analytics infrastructure may set strictly necessary cookies required to deliver the site securely.

6. How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run ChinaFit AI:

  • Creem — payment processing for the Lifetime Pro Pass.
  • DeepSeek — AI analysis of the measurements you submit for Pro reports.
  • Hosting / infrastructure providers (for example Vercel) — to serve the website and API.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of ChinaFit AI or our users.

7. Data retention

Measurement data is processed to produce a recommendation and is not used to build a long-term user profile. Server logs are retained only as long as needed for security and operations. You can clear the Pro token from your browser at any time by deleting site data in your browser settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email support@chinafitai.com. We will respond within a reasonable period.

9. International transfers

ChinaFit AI is offered globally. Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country, including by our payment, AI, and hosting providers. By using the service, you understand that your information may be transferred to those locations.

10. Children’s privacy

ChinaFit AI is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the website after an update means you accept the revised policy.
