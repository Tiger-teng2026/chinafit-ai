Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026 · Also included in our Terms of Service

This Acceptable Use Policy explains how you may and may not use ChinaFit AI, including the free size calculator and the paid AI sizing reports (the “AI sizing tool”). By using the website you agree to this policy and our Terms of Service.

Questions or abuse reports: support@chinafitai.com.

1. What the AI sizing tool is for

ChinaFit AI is a consumer shopping aid. It converts body or garment-related measurements into estimated clothing and shoe sizes for Chinese marketplaces (currently Taobao, JD.com, Poizon/Dewu, and 1688) and, for Pro users, generates a reference report and a seller measurement message.

Outputs are estimates only. They are not a guaranteed fit, not medical or health advice, and not a professional tailoring service.

2. Permitted uses

You may use the AI sizing tool to:

  • Convert your own clothing or shoe measurements for personal cross-border shopping.
  • Compare a seller’s size chart with your measurements to choose a size.
  • Generate a personal AI sizing report and keep a copy for your own purchase decisions.
  • Send the generated seller message to a marketplace seller only to confirm product measurements, fabric, last, or grading.
  • Help a friend or family member shop if they have consented to you entering their measurements.

3. Prohibited uses

You must not use the AI sizing tool, the website, or any generated output to:

  • Commit fraud, scams, impersonation, or any other illegal activity.
  • Harass, threaten, defame, or mislead a person, seller, or business.
  • Generate content unrelated to clothing or shoe sizing (for example essays, malware, adult content, political propaganda, or jailbroken/unrelated AI chat).
  • Attempt to jailbreak, prompt-inject, or otherwise override the AI so it produces off-topic, harmful, or prohibited content.
  • Present AI size recommendations as a medical, nutritional, body-image, or health diagnosis, or use the tool to assess or score people’s bodies beyond shopping fit.
  • Enter another person’s body measurements without their consent, or collect children’s data (under 16) through the tool.
  • Submit government IDs, payment-card numbers, health diagnoses, or other sensitive personal data we do not request.
  • Resell, white-label, or commercially redistribute AI reports as if they were your own sizing product without our written permission.
  • Scrape, overload, reverse engineer, bypass paywalls or rate limits, or use bots to mass-generate reports except through interfaces we expressly provide.
  • Upload malware or interfere with the security or availability of the site or API.
  • Use generated seller messages to impersonate ChinaFit AI, a marketplace, or another customer.

4. AI-generated content rules

  • Pro reports are produced by a third-party model (DeepSeek) solely to support a size decision.
  • You remain responsible for checking the seller’s actual measurements before you buy.
  • You may not treat or advertise an AI recommendation as a guaranteed fit or as an official size from Taobao, Poizon, JD.com, or 1688.

5. Enforcement

If we reasonably believe you have violated this policy, we may refuse, suspend, or terminate access — including a Pro Pass — and report illegal activity to relevant providers or authorities. To report misuse, email support@chinafitai.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the AI sizing tool means you accept the revised policy.
