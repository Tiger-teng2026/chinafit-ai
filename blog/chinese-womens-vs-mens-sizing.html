Women’s Sizing vs. Men’s Sizing – The Unisex Confusion on Taobao

Last updated: September 3, 2026 · ~7 min read

You’re shopping on Taobao. You find a unisex hoodie — looks great, good price. The size chart says “chest 112cm” for L. You wear a men’s L in US sizes. You order the L.

When it arrives, the shoulders are narrow, the sleeves are short, and the chest feels tight. You check the listing again and realize the chart was for women’s measurements — or a “unisex” chart that was really a women’s chart with a new label. Chinese women’s vs men’s sizing is not labeled the way US shoppers expect, and 男女同款 (unisex) is a wild card.

A Chinese L is not one L

Chinese size charts do not always say whether they are for men or women. When they do, the conversion still is not 1:1 with the US. A Chinese women’s L is not a Chinese men’s L — and neither matches a US L. Letter sizes already run 1–2 sizes small; see Chinese L vs US L. Gender cut is the second trap on top of that.

Chinese women’s clothing is cut for narrower shoulders, a smaller bust, and a shorter torso than Western women’s clothing. Chinese men’s clothing still has narrower shoulders and chests than US men’s fits. So:

  • A woman buying “unisex” that was designed for Asian men may still get a tight shoulder and short sleeve.
  • A man buying “unisex” that was designed for Asian women often gets something that fits like a women’s crop.

Guys order unisex hoodies thinking they got a deal and receive a women’s-cut top. Women order “men’s” jackets for an oversized look and still find the shoulders too narrow and the sleeves too short.

Label on the listing Typical US-equivalent fit
Chinese men’s LAbout US men’s S or M — leaner, shorter sleeve
Chinese women’s LAbout US women’s S or XS — smaller bust and hip
Unisex / 男女同款 LWild card. Often Asian men’s, sometimes women’s with a new name
Chest 96cm, no genderAssume the smaller cut. Confirm if 96cm is flat or circumference

These are typical factory patterns, not a guarantee. Always match the listing’s centimeters.

Why “chest 112cm” still feels tight

The number on the chart can look like a US large. Two problems: it may be a flat measurement you still have to interpret, and the pattern is still Asian — narrower across the back and shorter in the sleeve even when chest circumference is close. A Western men’s L is not just a bigger chest. It is a different shoulder slope and armhole.

Some sellers do not label charts at all. You see “Size: M, Chest: 96cm” with no 男 or 女. Because 96cm looks like a US small/medium body chest, you assume it is your size. If that 96cm is pit-to-pit doubled with zero ease — or a women’s body chest — the hoodie will not fit like the Champion L in your closet.

How to size unisex, men’s, and women’s listings

  1. Look for 男 / men or 女 / women on the chart. If they are not there, assume the smaller of the two — often women’s — and size from centimeters. Do not assume “unisex” means US unisex.
  2. Ignore the letter size. Compare chest (or 平铺胸围), shoulder width (肩宽), and sleeve length (袖长) to a hoodie or jacket you already own that fits. If the chart’s chest is smaller than your favorite shirt’s chest, it will not fit — men’s, women’s, or unisex.
  3. Do not size down on Chinese men’s just because you want “not oversized.” Chinese men’s already runs small vs US men’s. Women who want a true oversized look from a Chinese men’s listing should still match measurements (and often still size up vs their US women’s letter). Men buying women’s or women’s-as-unisex should plan on going up two letters and checking shoulder width — letters will not fix a women’s shoulder.
  4. Read reviews and buyer photos. If a guy says “I’m 5'10" and this fits tight in the shoulders,” you know the cut. Photos of Western buyers are more useful than the gender word on the title.

FAQ

Can a woman wear Chinese men’s sizing for an oversized fit?

Sometimes, if the chest, shoulder, and length in cm actually match an oversized garment you like. The men’s letter being “bigger” than the women’s letter on the same shop is not enough — Chinese men’s is still smaller than US men’s.

What does 男女同款 mean?

It means “same style for men and women.” It does not mean a US unisex block. Treat it as unlabeled and trust 肩宽 and 袖长.

Should men ever buy Chinese women’s sizes?

Only if the centimeter chart matches a piece you already wear. A women’s XL will not become a men’s M because you want it to. Shoulders and sleeve length are the usual deal-breakers.
