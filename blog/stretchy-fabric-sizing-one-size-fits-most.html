Stretchy Fabric Sizing – Why “One Size Fits Most” Fails on Taobao and AliExpress

Last updated: September 3, 2026 · ~7 min read

You see a cute top on AliExpress or Taobao. The fabric is 95% polyester and 5% spandex — stretchy, forgiving, comfortable. The size chart says “S fits 80–90cm bust.” You measure yourself: 88cm. Perfect, right?

Not so fast. Stretchy fabric sizing on Chinese listings is one of the most reliable ways to order a top that technically goes on and still looks terrible.

Stretch does not mean the size chart is generous

Sellers often take the smallest unstretched measurement and call that the “fits” number. They lay the garment flat, measure armpit to armpit, and write 80cm. When you put it on, that same top can stretch to 95cm or more. That sounds like extra room. It is not.

Chinese stretchy clothes are cut for smaller frames. The spandex is there so a slightly wider range can physically get the garment on — not so a Western M or L can wear an Asian S in comfort. If you are at the top of that “fits” range, you will fight the fabric the whole time you wear it.

Those chart numbers are also usually flat garment measurements, not body circumference. Double a pit-to-pit number before you compare it to your bust, then add ease. Stretch is not a substitute for that math.

The stuffed-sausage test

A stretchy bodysuit from a Chinese seller said M fit bust 84–92cm. Buyer at 90cm — right in range. It went on. It was not wearable. The fabric was stretched so thin you could see through it. Sure, it “fit” by the numbers. It was still a return you cannot easily return from Taobao or 1688.

Rule of thumb: if you need the stretch to make the garment reach your body, you are already too big for that cut. Stretch should be recovery and movement, not the only reason the zipper closes.

What “one size fits most” actually means

On Taobao, AliExpress, and 1688, “one size fits most” is often 均码 (Free Size) with a different English label. It is not a Western one-size. It is a single pattern graded for a typical Asian women’s frame. If you are outside that range, stretch will not save you. It will make the garment look and feel wrong.

How to size stretchy Chinese clothes

  1. Ignore the letter size. Look for unstretched flat measurements — armpit to armpit, shoulder to hem, waist laid flat. Compare them to a stretchy item you already own that fits. If the listing’s flats are smaller than your favorite shirt, the spandex will not magically add length in the shoulders or torso.
  2. Read overseas reviews. On stretchy items, buyers say exactly how the fabric behaves: “runs small, even with stretch,” “fits true to size but fabric is thin,” “ordered up one size and glad I did.” Those comments beat any size chart.
  3. If you are between sizes, size up. Stretch will take up slack if the piece is a little large. If it is too small, you are stuck with a garment that technically fits and feels terrible. This is the opposite of “order your usual size because it stretches.”

FAQ

Can I order my usual US size if the fabric has spandex?

No. A Chinese S/M/L is still cut smaller, and stretch is not ease. Match unstretched flats to a garment you own, then size up if you sit at the top of the range.

Is AliExpress “one size fits most” the same as US one-size?

Almost never. Treat it as 均码 unless the listing shows chest, shoulder, and length in cm that match a piece you already wear.

Does stretch change how I read a flat chest measurement?

You still double pit-to-pit to get circumference. Do not subtract ease just because the fabric stretches. You want wearing ease in the unstretched garment, not a painted-on skin.
