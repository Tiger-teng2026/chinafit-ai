Taobao Size Chart: Complete Guide for US/UK Shoppers

Last updated: August 24, 2026

When shopping on Taobao, one of the biggest challenges for international buyers is understanding Chinese size charts. Chinese sizes often run smaller than US/UK sizes, and the measurements are usually given in centimeters. This guide will help you decode Taobao size charts and find the right fit.

Why Chinese Sizes Are Different

Chinese clothing is tailored for Asian body proportions, which tend to be smaller in the shoulders and chest. A Chinese "L" may fit like a Western "M" or even "S". Always refer to the flat measurements provided by the seller, not just the letter size. If you have ever matched your body chest to the chart and still gotten a painted-on shirt, read flat measurements vs. body measurements.

How to Convert Taobao Clothing Sizes

Most Taobao listings include a size chart with measurements in centimeters. To convert, you'll need your own body measurements. Use a soft measuring tape to measure your chest, shoulders, sleeve length, and total length. Then compare with the seller's chart.

  • Chest: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms.
  • Shoulder width: Measure from one shoulder seam to the other across your back.
  • Sleeve length: Measure from your shoulder seam down to your wrist.
  • Total length: Measure from the highest point of your shoulder down to where you want the garment to end.

How to Convert Chinese Shoe Sizes

Chinese shoe sizes usually use foot length in millimeters. For example, 260 mm is about EU 42 / US Men's 8.5. Always check the insole length provided by the seller, not just the labeled size.
