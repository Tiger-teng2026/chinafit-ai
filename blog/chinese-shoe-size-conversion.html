Chinese Shoe Size Conversion: CN to US/EU/UK Chart

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Chinese shoe sizes are usually labeled by foot length in millimeters or by a "CN" numeric size. This can be confusing for international shoppers because a Chinese 42 may not exactly match a US 9 or EU 42 depending on the brand. This guide explains how to convert Chinese shoe sizes accurately.

Why Insole Length Is the Most Reliable Metric

Many Chinese sellers list the "CN size" but the actual insole length may be shorter or longer than expected. The only way to be sure is to ask the seller for the insole length in millimeters. Compare that to your own foot length plus 5–10 mm of toe room.

Platform Differences: Taobao, 1688, Poizon

  • Taobao / 1688: Budget and unbranded shoes often run small. A CN 42 may have an insole of only 255–260 mm. Always request insole length.
  • Poizon / Dewu: Authentic branded shoes (Nike, Adidas, New Balance) follow standard brand sizing. Check the official brand size chart.
  • JD.com: Official brand stores are more reliable, but third-party sellers can be inconsistent.
